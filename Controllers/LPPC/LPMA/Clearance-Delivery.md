# Clearance Delivery
- MACTR (Tower) is responsible for validating new flight plans from LPMA, issuing enroute clearances and takes part in managing and enforcing the departure sequence.
- DCL system is not available at Madeira, therefore, clearances must be obtained via voice, not PDC.
- All procedures, except GALOZ and NIDUL, initial climb FL60. GALOZ and NIDUL initial climb FL100.
- VFR Circuits are permitted. However, they are highly discouraged as they create conflicts with arriving IFR traffic.
- VFR departures that intend to leave the CTR must always be coordinated with the position directly above.

# Phraseology
## IFR Clearance
- "[Callsign], information [ATIS Letter], QNH [QNH], cleared to [destination], [SID] departure, climb [initial climb], squawk [code]."
- "[Callsign], readback correct, report ready."
- "[Callsign], negative, [correction]."
## VFR Clearance
- **Circuits**: "[Callsign], information [ATIS Letter], QNH [QNH], cleared from Madeira to Madeira, after departure join [circuit leg], climb to [altitude], squawk [code]."
- **Cross-Country**: "[Callsign], information [ATIS Letter], QNH [QNH], cleared from Madeira to [destination], after departure proceed to [visual reference point], climb to [altitude], squawk [code]."
## Frequencies
- 124.660 MHz (LPMA_TWR) - one two four **decimal** six six zero
- 119.605 MHz (LPMA_APP) - one one niner **decimal** six zero five
- 132.555 MHz (LPPC_CTR) - one three two **decimal** five five five
- 118.100 MHz - one one eight **decimal** one
## Altimeter Setting
- QNH 1009 - "QNH one zero zero niner"
- QNH 1000 - "QNH one thousand"
- QNH 993 - "QNH niner niner three"
## Altitude
- Levels such as FL100, FL200, FL300 are said "One Hundred / Two Hundred / Three Hundred".
## Common Words
- **ACKNOWLEDGE** = Have you __received and understood__ the message?
- **AFFIRM** = Yes
- **APPROVED** = __Permission__ for the specific action that has been __granted__
- **BREAK** = To __separate__ two distinct portions of information within the same transmission
- **BREAK BREAK** = Indicates thhe __separation__ between messages transmited __to different aircraft__ in a busy environment. __Not to be used when a read-back is mandatory__!
- **CANCEL** = Cancel the previous clearance
- **CONFIRM** = __Request for verification__ of a clearance, instruction, action, information, etc.
- **CORRECT** = True/accurate
- **CORRECTION** = An error has been made in this transmission. The __following__ transmission __is the correct version__
- **DISREGARD** = Ignore
- **I SAY AGAIN** = __I repeat__ the instruction/message for clarity of emphasis
- **NEGATIVE** = No / permission not granted / that is not correct
- **READ BACK** = __Repeat__ all, or the specified part of this message, __back to me__ exactly as received
- **REPORT** = Report the requested information
- **REQUEST** = I would __like to know__ / wish to obtain
- **ROGER** = I have __received__ your last transmission. __This does not mean "yes"__!
- **SAY AGAIN** = __Repeat all__, or the specified part of your message
- **STANDBY** = __Wait and I will call you__. Should not be read-back
- **UNABLE** = I __cannot comply__ with your request/instrction/clearance
- **WILCO** = I __understand__ your message __and will comply__ with it
