# LPPC_CTR - Enroute
---
## Flight Routing
### Arrivals to Portugal

Aircraft entering Portuguese airspace with an aerodrome within Portugal as their destination should, traffic permitting, be cleared DCT the first point of the published STAR and subsequently cleared for the appropriate STAR.
### Overflights

Aircraft transiting Portuguese airspace should, traffic permitting, be cleared DCT the appropriate sector boundary/exit point.
### Flight Levels

The magnetic track-based cruising level rule does not apply to flights operating entirely within Portugal.

 For applicable flights:
- **Northbound**: Even flight levels (100, 120, 140, etc...)
- **Southbound**: Odd flight levels (110, 130, 150, etc...)
### Coordination & Handoffs
**Approach handoff**: Aircraft should normally be transferred to the appropriate Approach sector between FL250 and FL300, traffic permitting.

**Adjacent FIR/ACC Handoff**: Aircraft entering an adjacent FIR/ACC should be transferred to the appropriate adjacent Centre no later than 10 NM before the sector boundary, traffic permitting.
## Phraseology

- "[Callsign], expected approach time (HHMM), adjust speed accordingly."
- "[Callsign], expect hold at [FIX]."
- "[Callsign], at [FIX], hold as published." // "[Callsign], hold at [FIX], inbound track [track], [left/right] hand pattern, outbound time [time]."
- "[Callsign], reduce/maintain speed [kts]."
- "[Callsign], Lisboa Control, identified."
