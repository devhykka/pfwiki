# Local Control Responsibilities

The Local Control controller's **primary responsibility is to control operations on the active runway(s) and aircraft in immediate vicinity of the airport.** Local Control is responsible for authorizing aircraft to take off, land, enter, cross, or otherwise use an active runway, while maintaining the required separation between aircraft and coordinating with other controllers as necessary.

# Local Control Phraseology

Fairbanks International Airport follows FAA phraseology, though PFATC does not require specific phraseology, so you may use other phraseologies such as CAA and ICAO.

!!!info Note 
In all examples the controller identifies themselves each time. In practice you only must do this once to establish two radio communication.

### Takeoff Clearance

**RUNWAY (number), CLEARED FOR TAKEOFF.**

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Tower, Runway Two-Zero Right, cleared for takeoff.

**RUNWAY (number), WIND (surface wind in direction and velocity), CLEARED FOR TAKEOFF.**

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Tower, Runway Two-Zero Right, wind two-one-zero at eight, cleared for takeoff.

**Intersection Departure**


**RUNWAY (number) AT (taxiway), CLEARED FOR TAKEOFF.**

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Tower, Runway Two-Zero Right at Bravo, cleared for takeoff.

!!!info NOTE
If multiple aircraft are waiting for departure and one is at the full length while another is at an intersection, specify FULL LENGTH when clearing the full-length aircraft.

**RUNWAY (number), FULL LENGTH, CLEARED FOR TAKEOFF.**

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Tower, Runway Two-Zero Right, full length, cleared for takeoff.

### Line Up and Wait

Line Up and Wait (LUAW) may be used when appropriate.

**RUNWAY (number), LINE UP AND WAIT.**

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Tower, Runway Two-Zero Right, line up and wait.

When issuing LUAW, ensure the runway is suitable for the operation and that the aircraft can be safely positioned on the runway without creating a conflict with other traffic.


### Landing Clearance

Landing aircraft must receive a landing clearance before landing.

**RUNWAY (number), CLEARED TO LAND.**

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Tower, Runway Two-Zero Right, cleared to land.

**RUNWAY (number), WIND (surface wind in direction and velocity), CLEARED TO LAND.**

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Tower, Runway Two-Zero Right, wind two-one-zero at eight, cleared to land.

### Arrival Sequence

**ENTER (traffic pattern leg).**

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Tower, enter left downwind Runway Two-Zero Right.

**MAKE (traffic pattern leg).**

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Tower, make right traffic Runway Two-Zero Right.

**CONTINUE.**

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Tower, continue, number two following a Boeing 737 on final.

**REPORT (position).**

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Tower, report two-mile final.


### Traffic Information


**TRAFFIC (description), (position), (direction/movement).**

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Tower, traffic, Boeing 737, two-mile final Runway Two-Zero Right.

**TRAFFIC (description), (relative position).**

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Tower, traffic, Cessna 172 on left downwind to your right.


### Runway Crossing


**CROSS (runway) AT (point/intersection).**

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Tower, cross Runway Two-Zero Right at Alpha.

When Ground Control requests a runway crossing, Local Control must authorize the crossing before Ground issues the instruction to the aircraft.

!!! info NOTE

Runway crossings must be explicit. Do not assume that an aircraft is authorized to cross a runway simply because its taxi route continues across it.

Aircraft require explicit authorization to cross a runway, including inactive or closed runways.


### Go-Around / Balked Landing

If an aircraft cannot safely land, Local Control may instruct the aircraft to go around.

**GO AROUND.**

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Tower, go around.

Additional instructions may be provided as necessary:

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Tower, go around, fly runway heading.

_or_

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Tower, go around, make left traffic.

### Touch-and-Go / Stop-and-Go / Low Approach


**RUNWAY (number), CLEARED TOUCH-AND-GO.**

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Tower, Runway Two-Zero Right, cleared touch-and-go.

**RUNWAY (number), CLEARED STOP-AND-GO.**

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Tower, Runway Two-Zero Right, cleared stop-and-go.


**CLEARED LOW APPROACH.**

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Tower, Runway Two-Zero Right, cleared low approach.
