# Ground Responsibilities
The Ground controller's primary responsibility is to **control aircraft and vehicles on the airport's movement area, excluding the active runway(s).** Ground is responsible for issuing taxi instructions, ensuring aircraft and vehicles maintain proper separation, and coordinating with other controllers as necessary.

Ground should ensure that aircraft are given appropriate taxi routes and holding instructions when necessary, while also ensuring that aircraft do not enter or cross an active runway without authorization from the appropriate controller.

Ground is also responsible for **coordinating with Clearance Delivery and Local Control** when necessary to ensure aircraft are properly sequenced and that taxi instructions do not conflict with other airport operations.


# Ground Phrasology

Fairbanks International Airport follows FAA phraseology, though PFATC does not require specific phraseology, so you may use other phraseologies such as CAA and ICAO.

In the United States the ramp is uncontrolled.

!!!info Note 
In all examples the controller identifies themselves each time. In practice you only must do this once to establish two radio communication.

### TAXI to Runway

**RUNWAY (number), TAXI VIA (route as necessary).**

_or_

**RUNWAY (number), TAXI VIA (route as necessary)(hold short instructions as necessary).**

!!! info NOTE 
If the specific taxi route ends into a connecting taxiway with the same identifier (for example, taxiway “A” connects with Taxiway “A1”) at the approach end of the runway, the connecting taxiway may be omitted from the clearance.

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Ground, Runway Two-Zero Right, taxi via Alpha

_or_

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Ground, Runway Two-Zero Right, taxi via Bravo, Alpha, hold short of Runway Two-Zero Right at Bravo.

_or_

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Ground, Runway Two-Zero Right, taxi via Bravo, Alpha, cross Runway Two-Zero Right at Bravo.



!!! info NOTE
Do not use the word “cleared” in conjunction with authorization for aircraft to taxi. Use the prefix “taxi,” “proceed,” or “hold,” as appropriate, for aircraft instructions and “proceed” or “hold” for equipment/vehicles/personnel.

### TAXI to

**TAXI to (destination) VIA (route as necessary).**

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Ground, taxi to the ramp via Alpha.

!!! info NOTE
The absence of holding instructions authorizes an aircraft cross all taxiways that intersect the taxi route.

### Holding
To hold an aircraft where they are:

**HOLD POSITION.**

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Ground, hold position.

To hold an aircraft at a location:

**HOLD SHORT OF (location).**

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Ground, hold short of Alpha.

### Behind / Follow

**BEHIND (traffic) TAXI VIA (route as necessary).**

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Ground, behind the American Airlines Boeing 737 passing right to left on Alpha taxi via Alpha.

**FOLLOW (traffic) (restrictions as necessary)**

Alaska 123, Fairbanks Ground, Runway Two-Zero Right follow the American Airlines Boeing 737 passing right to left on Alpha.

!!! info NOTE
Do not use the words BEHIND and FOLLOW in the same instruction. FOLLOW BEHIND is not to be used.
