## ATIS

### Preferred Runway Modes

Runway 27 is the preferred runway, however the most into the wind runway should always be selected.

### ATIS Format

> EGLC COM ATIS [ATISCODE]. [TIME]. RWY IN USE [RWY NO.]. TRANSITION LEVEL FLIGHT LEVEL [TL]. [ARPT_COND] [NOTAMS] [WX]. ACKNOWLEDGE RECEIPT OF INFORMATION [ATISCODE] AND ADVISE AIRCRAFT TYPE ON FIRST CONTACT.
