## ATIS

### Preferred Runway Modes

Use of runway 26R/08L is not to be simulated in PFATC so runway 26L/08R should be used at all times. Runway 26L is the preferred runway when the tailwind component is less than 5 knots and the runway surface is dry. 

### ATIS Format

> [FACILITY] COM ATIS [ATISCODE]. [OBSTIME]. RWY IN USE [RWY]. [TL]. ILS APPROACH TO BE EXPECTED. (GATWICK DELIVERY, GROUND AND TOWER ARE CLOSED. ATC SERVICE CONTINUES ON GATWICK DIRECTOR 126.825)* ***As required***. [ARPT_COND]. [NOTAMS]. [WX]. ACKNOWLEDGE RECEIPT OF INFORMATION [ATISCODE] AND ADVISE AIRCRAFT TYPE ON FIRST CONTACT.
