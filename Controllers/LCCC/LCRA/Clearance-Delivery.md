# Clearance Delivery (CD) — Controller Guide

**Position:** Combined into Ground at military aerodromes (no standalone Clearence frequency unless locally specified)
**Applies to:** IFR and VFR departures requiring a clearance
**Source:** PFATC Military Phraseology Manual v1.0, Section 1 — Aerodrome Control

---

## 1.1.1 Engine Start

Aircraft must provide their POB (Number of persons onboard) and parking position on start request. Local ATIS may dictate other details to be passed.

Squadron-based aircraft are not required to request start (excluding Akrotiri/Brize Norton), so details are passed in the taxi request.

| Role | Phraseology |
|---|---|
| Aircraft | Akrotiri Ground, TARTAN59, bay 3, request start, 9 POB. |
| ATC Unit | TARTAN59, Akrotiri Ground, start, runway 36 in use, QFE 999 hPa, Regional 1001, outside air +15 degrees |
| Aircraft | Start, runway 36, QFE 999 hPa, Regional 1001, TARTAN59 |

**Note:** If POB is not given on initial contact, request it at the end of your transmission with *"…Request your POB"*.

---

## 1.1.2 Taxi

Aircraft should request taxi when ready. At military aerodromes, IFR/VFR clearance is given at the holding point.

| Role | Phraseology |
|---|---|
| Aircraft | TARTAN59 request taxi, information B from bay 3, 9 POB |
| ATC Unit | TARTAN59, Akrotiri Ground, runway 28 in use, QFE 999 hPa, Regional 1001 taxi to 28 hold, runway 28 via Y |
| Aircraft | Taxi to 28 hold, runway 28 via Y TARTN59 |

**Note:** Provide ATIS code and/or QFE if it has changed since the start clearance.

---

## 1.1.3 Climbout Details

An aircraft's climbout details refer to its intentions after departure, as they were not given on start. This is only asked to aircraft without a flight plan (VFR), as most IFR aircraft have flight plans with clear routes. It can also be used for IFR aircraft with variable routes, such as that of "OPR IFR".

| Role | Phraseology |
|---|---|
| ATC Unit | JIGSAW91 request your climb out details |
| Aircraft | Request low level to the north |

---

## 1.1.4 VFR Clearances

VFR clearances must be coordinated with Tower. Approach should be notified of this. IFR clearances to leave controlled airspace must be coordinated with Approach, notifying Tower.

| Role | Phraseology |
|---|---|
| ATC Unit | JIGSAW91 cleared to leave the MATZ, low level to the North, not above height 1000FT QFE 999 hPa, squawk 3701 |
| Aircraft | Cleared to leave the MATZ, low level to the North, not above height 1000FT QFE 999 hPa, squawk 3701, JIGSAW91 |

---

## Quick Reference

- **Readback order:** ATC leads with callsign; aircraft readbacks end with callsign.
- **Squadron-based aircraft** (excluding Akrotiri/Brize Norton): no separate start request — details folded into the taxi request.
- **VFR clearance:** coordinate with Tower, notify Approach.
- **IFR clearance to leave CAS:** coordinate with Approach, notify Tower.
- Clearance is issued **at the holding point**, not as a separate pre-taxi CD call.
